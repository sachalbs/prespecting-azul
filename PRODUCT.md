# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

B2B sales teams (SDRs, Heads of Sales, RevOps) and founders running their own
outbound. Their context: outbound reply rates have collapsed because inboxes are
saturated with barely-personalized templated mail. The job they need done is to
earn more *qualified replies* without burning their domain reputation or hiring
another SDR. They evaluate tools skeptically and distrust hype.

## Product Purpose

Azul is an autonomous AI sales rep (SDR). It researches every prospect in depth
(funding, hiring, stack, intent signals), writes a genuinely personal message,
and optimizes for **reply rate, not send volume**. It is managed like an
employee, piloted from Slack / WhatsApp / Teams with no dashboard to learn, and
prospects via email + LinkedIn. The site's single job: earn a qualified
early-access waitlist signup. Success = a visitor trusts the premise enough to
join.

## Brand Personality

Professional, confident, concrete. Swiss/brutalist precision: it looks like an
instrument, not a pitch deck. Speaks plainly (vouvoiement in FR), zero jargon,
no AI clichés. The confidence is shown through restraint and specificity, never
through superlatives.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS gradient landing pages (purple gradients, glassmorphism, hero-stat
  template, identical icon-card grids).
- Fake credibility: invented client logos, fabricated stats, made-up testimonials,
  "trusted by 10,000 teams" with no teams.
- The "first wave AI SDR" positioning it argues against: volume, blast, "more
  sends," spray-and-pray.

## Design Principles

1. **Total honesty.** No social proof we don't have. Early-access means we say
   early-access. Credibility comes from precision, not claims.
2. **Show the work.** Demonstrate the product's value (a real researched message,
   the research-to-reply process) instead of asserting it.
3. **Reply rate is the hero.** Every section ladders back to the one metric that
   matters; volume is the foil.
4. **Hold the line.** One ink, one cobalt accent, hairline rules. Restraint reads
   as expert confidence; decoration reads as insecurity.
5. **Bilingual, English-principal.** EN is the default/canonical; FR is a
   first-class peer, never a degraded translation.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA: body text ≥4.5:1, large text ≥3:1. Visible keyboard focus on every
interactive element. Full `prefers-reduced-motion` support (the WebGL blob,
cursor trail, ripple, and scroll reveals all degrade gracefully). Touch targets
≥44px; the cursor trail is disabled on touch/no-hover devices.
